      Myths and facts

      Myth: Indoor plants clean smoggy air.
      Fact: You would need far more plants than any home holds to matter. A sealed room with a filter does much more.
      Myth: A cloth scarf is as good as a mask.
      Fact: Fine PM2.5 passes through cloth. A fitted N95 or FFP2 filters at least 95% of fine particles.
      Myth: Open the windows to freshen the air.
      Fact: On smog days that lets pollution in. Ventilate briefly at midday when readings are lowest.
      Myth: One household cannot change the air.
      Fact: Open burning, vehicles and cooking fuel add up street by street. Lane-level action is measurable.

      Frequently asked questions

      Where do I find today's AQI?

      Use your national air quality app or website, or a trusted global AQI service. Enter the number into the planner for advice that matches today.

      Is this medical advice?

      No. It gives general guidance. Speak to a doctor about symptoms, medicines or a health condition.

      Are the impact numbers exact?

      No. Figures marked as estimates are approximate. The High, Medium and Low labels show relative benefit.

      Is my data stored?

      No. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is saved. If AI is enabled, its key stays on the server and never reaches your browser.
